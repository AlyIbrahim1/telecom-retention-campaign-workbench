## Design Context

### Users

The application is for English-speaking, non-technical e& Egypt employees who plan and operate customer-retention campaigns. They need to find risky customers, understand the account facts behind each record, build a capacity-limited outreach list, run individual or bulk predictions, and use a conversational assistant without learning ML terminology.

The main job is to turn a large customer table into a small, reviewable campaign list. Users may be working quickly and comparing many similar records, so the interface must favor clarity, traceability, and confident decisions over decorative analytics.

### Brand Personality

Digitally confident, customer-first, and energetic. The tone is direct and businesslike without feeling cold. It reflects e& Egypt's public emphasis on being a leading digital telecom brand, delivering segmented services, and improving customer experience and value.

Reference: [e& Egypt — About Us](https://www.eand.com.eg/portal/about_us_en.html)

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, operational workspace inspired by e&'s bold identity: strong typography, generous neutral surfaces, restrained use of a vivid red accent, dark readable text, and crisp data visualization. The interface should feel like a focused campaign workbench, not a generic card dashboard or a consumer marketing site.

Use only official brand assets and exact brand tokens if the project owner supplies them. Until then, use clearly replaceable design tokens rather than claiming unofficial colors or fonts are brand-standard. English and left-to-right layout are sufficient for the MVP. Desktop is the primary context, with usable tablet and mobile adaptations.

### Design Principles

1. **Decisions before decoration:** emphasize customer ranking, campaign capacity, and the next review action.
2. **Explain every score:** keep churn risk, value, priority, selection status, model version, and scoring time distinct and visible.
3. **Progressive disclosure:** show a concise customer list first and reveal full account details, history, or chatbot context only when requested.
4. **Human control:** predictions and chatbot suggestions inform decisions; users confirm customer creation, updates, and campaign selection.
5. **Trust through states:** design explicit loading, empty, validation, stale, partial-success, retry, and conflict states.
6. **Accessible by default:** target WCAG 2.2 AA, full keyboard use, visible focus, non-color status cues, readable tables, and reduced motion.
